# Challenge 12 — Revenue Assurance Analytics

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Lower losses, healthier meters and better collections.

## Problem Statement

For Telangana's electricity distribution utilities, sound revenue collection and energy accounting are important to financial sustainability. Relevant evidence is spread across billing, metering, collections, GIS and SCADA systems, making it difficult to identify theft, malfunctioning meters and geographically concentrated collection weaknesses. Integrated analytics can help utilities distinguish unusual patterns and direct verification teams to priority cases.

## Solution Sought (official)

Build AI analytics across billing, metering, collections, GIS and SCADA data to detect potential theft, flag faulty meters and identify areas with weak collections.

## Expected Impact (official)

Lower commercial losses, healthier metering systems and improved collection performance.
