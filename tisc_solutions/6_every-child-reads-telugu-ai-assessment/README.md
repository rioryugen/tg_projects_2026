# Challenge 6 — Every Child Reads: Telugu AI Assessment

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** School Education
**Departments:** School Education
**Impact statement:** Better foundational literacy in every government school.

## Problem Statement

Foundational literacy is a priority for Telangana schools, but periodic paper assessments can provide limited, delayed feedback on individual reading gaps. Teachers managing varied learning levels need more frequent and actionable insights, including for Telugu and English reading proficiency.

## Solution Sought (official)

Build a voice-enabled Telugu and English reading assessment that scores proficiency promptly and recommends personalised practice and teacher interventions.

## Expected Impact (official)

Support timely teaching interventions and measurable gains in foundational literacy.
