# Challenge 9 — Drone Based Monitoring

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Faster grid asset inspection and prioritised field action.

## Problem Statement

Telangana's power lines, feeders and substations extend across urban, industrial and rural terrain. Physical inspection of this dispersed infrastructure is time-consuming, and defects such as conductor sag, corrosion, vegetation encroachment and thermal hotspots may be missed until they threaten service continuity. Utilities need faster, more consistent condition assessments and a way to prioritise field attention by severity.

## Solution Sought (official)

Use drone surveys of feeders, lines and substations, with AI analysis to detect cracks, sag, corrosion, vegetation and thermal hotspots and rank identified defects by severity.

## Expected Impact (official)

Faster grid-asset inspections and better prioritisation of field maintenance and corrective action.
