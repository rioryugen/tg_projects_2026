# Challenge 7 — Mapping Every Crop, Every Season

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Agriculture
**Departments:** Agriculture, Revenue
**Impact statement:** Better agricultural planning, procurement, insurance and scheme delivery.

## Problem Statement

Telangana's agricultural planning depends on timely crop booking at land-parcel level. Manual collection can be slow or inaccurate, affecting procurement, insurance, input supply and scheme administration. Diverse seasonal cropping patterns require rapid verification soon after sowing.

## Solution Sought (official)

Identify crops at survey-number level from satellite imagery, verified through farmer-facing mobile workflows, with early crop-stress signals where feasible.

## Expected Impact (official)

Improve crop records, procurement planning, insurance integrity and agricultural decision-making.
