# Challenge 14 — Auto Connection Cost Estimator

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Faster, transparent new-connection estimates.

## Problem Statement

Consumers seeking new electricity connections in Telangana need clear information on likely connection requirements and costs. Estimation can depend on the requested load, distance to nearby poles and the quantities of materials and work required. Manual or opaque estimation processes can create uncertainty and additional follow-up for applicants.

## Solution Sought (official)

Build a bilingual AI-assisted estimator where a consumer enters GPS location and required load; the system maps the nearest pole, generates a bill of quantities and estimates connection cost through a conversational interface.

## Expected Impact (official)

Faster, more transparent and consistent estimates for new electricity connections.
