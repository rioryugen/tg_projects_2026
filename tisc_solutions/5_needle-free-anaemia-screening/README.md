# Challenge 5 — Needle-Free Anaemia Screening

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Health
**Departments:** Health, Women Development & Child Welfare
**Impact statement:** Mass screening of pregnant women and adolescent girls.

## Problem Statement

Anaemia among pregnant women and adolescent girls remains a public-health priority in Telangana. Blood-based screening requires trained staff, consumables and repeat visits, which can constrain coverage in rural and underserved areas. Anganwadi centres and frontline health workers offer an important channel for more accessible screening.

## Solution Sought (official)

Develop a portable, affordable, clinically validated non-invasive haemoglobin screening device usable by ANMs and Anganwadi workers, with appropriate follow-up and referral workflows.

## Expected Impact (official)

Expand screening coverage, identify risk earlier and support preventive maternal and adolescent healthcare.
