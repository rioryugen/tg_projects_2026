# Challenge 17 — Storm / Weather Outage Prediction and Crew Dispatch

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Better SAIDI/SAIFI and faster restoration during monsoon and high winds.

## Problem Statement

Monsoon storms, strong winds and other severe weather can interrupt electricity supply across Telangana. Utilities must anticipate which sections of the network are most exposed and position field teams before faults occur. Reactive dispatch can lengthen restoration times and increase disruption for households, businesses and essential services.

## Solution Sought (official)

Combine weather forecasts and outage history to predict likely fault locations ahead of bad weather, then pre-position and sequence restoration crews.

## Expected Impact (official)

Improved outage duration and frequency performance (SAIDI/SAIFI) and faster restoration during monsoon storms and high winds.
