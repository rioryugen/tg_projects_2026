# Challenge 8 — Network Mapping, Sectionalizing and Load Switching

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Fewer overloaded DTRs and evidence-based investment decisions.

## Problem Statement

Telangana's distribution networks serve fast-growing urban neighbourhoods, industrial loads and dispersed rural communities. Feeder configurations, distribution transformer (DTR) loading and network connectivity can change as demand expands. Incomplete or fragmented network maps make it difficult for utilities to identify overloaded DTRs, assess feeder bifurcation and plan safe switching or conductor upgrades. Better network intelligence is needed to direct investment where it will improve reliability most.

## Solution Sought (official)

Develop AI-enabled feeder-network mapping and modelling that evaluates sectionalising and load-switching options, identifies overloaded assets and ranks where additional DTRs, feeder bifurcation or conductor upgrades are needed.

## Expected Impact (official)

Fewer overloaded DTRs, more reliable distribution and evidence-based network investment decisions.
