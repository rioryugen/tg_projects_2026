# Challenge 4 — Potholes, Fixed for Good

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Roads & Buildings
**Departments:** Roads & Buildings, Municipal Administration
**Impact statement:** Safer roads, faster repairs and reduced maintenance expenditure.

## Problem Statement

Roads in Hyderabad and other Telangana urban centres face heavy traffic, monsoon rainfall and repeated deterioration. Damage is often identified through complaints or manual inspection, and short-lived repairs increase costs and safety risks. A systematic approach is needed to locate, prioritise and permanently address road defects.

## Solution Sought (official)

Combine automated pothole mapping through cameras on buses and municipal vehicles with durable, economical cold-mix or other monsoon-resistant repair materials.

## Expected Impact (official)

Improve road safety and mobility, shorten repair times and reduce recurring maintenance expenditure.
