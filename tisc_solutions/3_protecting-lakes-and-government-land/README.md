# Challenge 3 — Protecting Lakes and Government Land

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Revenue
**Departments:** Revenue, Municipal Administration, Irrigation
**Impact statement:** Protect valuable government land, lakes and public assets.

## Problem Statement

Rapid urbanisation, particularly around Hyderabad, increases pressure on lakes, Full Tank Level boundaries and valuable government land. Encroachments may be detected only after construction has advanced, making intervention harder. Fragmented records and infrequent inspections constrain timely action, while lake loss affects flood resilience and groundwater recharge.

## Solution Sought (official)

Use monthly satellite change detection linked to land records and legally defined boundaries, with time-stamped evidence and alerts for new construction or land-use changes.

## Expected Impact (official)

Protect public assets and water bodies, improve early enforcement and strengthen environmental governance.
