# Challenge 1 — Solar-Powered Irrigation for Every Farm

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy, Agriculture
**Impact statement:** Reduce the ₹14,000 crore annual free-power burden.

## Problem Statement

Telangana's agricultural irrigation relies heavily on grid-powered pumps. Approximately 29 lakh agricultural pump connections and an estimated annual free-power subsidy of ₹14,000 crore create a substantial recurring burden for the state. Existing solar alternatives are costly, operate primarily during daylight and face theft and service challenges. Telangana's deep hard-rock borewells add technical demands, while free grid electricity gives farmers little incentive to switch.

## Solution Sought (official)

Develop 3–10 HP affordable solar or hybrid pumps, including retrofits for existing motors. Target at least 30% lower costs, metered grid backup and permitted surplus export, tamper-resistant energy accounting, dry-run and voltage protection, groundwater metering, theft-resistant installation, GPS tagging and dependable service.

## Expected Impact (official)

Reduce subsidy expenditure while providing farmers reliable irrigation, protecting groundwater and supporting clean-energy adoption.
