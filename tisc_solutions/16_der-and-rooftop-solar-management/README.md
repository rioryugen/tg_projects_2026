# Challenge 16 — DER and Rooftop-Solar Management

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Data-driven net-meter clearance, with saturated DTRs flagged in advance.

## Problem Statement

The expansion of rooftop solar and other distributed energy resources (DER) changes power flows on Telangana's distribution feeders. Local generation and net export vary with sunlight and consumer demand, while feeders and distribution transformers have practical limits on how much additional solar they can absorb safely. Utilities need feeder-level forecasting to manage growth without compromising network reliability.

## Solution Sought (official)

Forecast solar generation and net export, estimate how much additional rooftop or PM-KUSUM solar each feeder and DTR can safely accommodate, and flag likely saturation in advance.

## Expected Impact (official)

Data-driven net-metering clearance and earlier identification of distribution transformers approaching solar hosting limits.
