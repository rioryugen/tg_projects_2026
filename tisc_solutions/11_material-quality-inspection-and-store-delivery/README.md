# Challenge 11 — Material Quality Inspection and Store Delivery

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Quality verified before materials are accepted into stores.

## Problem Statement

Electricity infrastructure expansion and maintenance depend on reliable procurement and timely acceptance of materials. Telangana's utility stores handle equipment and components that must meet purchase-order specifications; manual checks against delivery records and technical requirements can be labour-intensive and inconsistent. Weak visibility at receipt can allow nonconforming materials into inventory and affect downstream project quality.

## Solution Sought (official)

Enable store officers to capture photographs and measurements of delivered materials and use AI to validate quality and conformity against purchase-order specifications recorded in SAP.

## Expected Impact (official)

Material quality verified before acceptance into utility stores, reducing avoidable defects and procurement risks.
