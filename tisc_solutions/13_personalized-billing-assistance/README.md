# Challenge 13 — Personalized Billing Assistance

> Telangana Innovation Startup Challenge (TISC) 2026 · source: https://tisc.telangana.gov.in/#challenges

**Sector:** Energy
**Departments:** Energy
**Impact statement:** Clearer bills and better-informed consumers.

## Problem Statement

Electricity bills can be difficult for Telangana consumers to interpret, especially when charges, consumption changes and tariff components are not readily understood. Consumers may also struggle to anticipate monthly expenses or identify why a bill differs from previous months. Accessible explanations in Telugu could improve transparency and reduce avoidable service queries.

## Solution Sought (official)

Enable consumers to photograph an electricity bill and receive an AI-generated explanation of each charge in Telugu, analysis of monthly consumption and a prediction of the next bill.

## Expected Impact (official)

Clearer electricity bills, better-informed consumers and improved understanding of energy use.
